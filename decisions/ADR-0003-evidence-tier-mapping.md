# ADR-0003: Mapping the sub-study's grades to the three app tiers

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-09-23)
- **Context:** The sub-study uses the grades "verified, emerging, unverified" plus "Emerging–moderate", "clinical consensus" and "low-quality but low risk". The app uses three tiers.
- **Decision:**
  - **Verified:** credentialed music therapy as an add-on (§8), and the sound-sensitivity strategies the sub-study calls "practical, supported by clinical consensus" (§2 table, §6). The UI adds "clinical consensus" as a sub-label where that applies.
  - **Emerging:** home music activities (§7.1), AMMT and NMT, and mechanism statements (§4).
  - **Unverified:** everything in §5.
  - A claim takes the tier of the practice it describes (see the claims register conventions).
- **Consequences:** Activities show "Emerging · low risk". The copy never calls home activities "therapy".
