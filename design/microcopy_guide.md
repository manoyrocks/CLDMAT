# Microcopy Guide

Owner: UX/UI Designers · Evaluators approve. Applies to all strings in `apps/web/src/i18n/en.json` and all content. The compliance checker enforces the banned list (REQ-AI-07).

## Voice
Warm, plain and brief. Write at about reading age 9 (grade 4–6). Use second person ("you", "your child"). Keep sentences to 20 words or fewer. One idea per sentence.

## Language
- **Identity-first and person-first language both appear** (principle 2.5): "autistic children" and "children with autism", varied naturally. Follow the family's lead where the app lets them set it.
- **Affirming:** we build communication, participation, wellbeing and enjoyment. We never "fix", "normalise" or "reduce autism".
- **Stimming:** harmless stimming is never a goal to reduce. Goal templates contain none.
- **Participation over performance:** "You played together 4 times this week" — not "Score: 60%".
- **No blame, no streak pressure:** "Any day you play together counts." Never "You broke your streak".

## Banned (content and UI)
cure, heal, rewire, recover or recovery, reverse autism, normalise, retrain the brain or hearing, clinically proven, proven to, guaranteed, miracle, detox. The only exception is the approved negation in D-001 and in Education Hub items with a recorded Evaluator negation approval.

## Patterns

| Situation | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | "Research suggests music therapy can help overall functioning in the short term." | "Music therapy treats autism." |
| Unverified | "There are no good trials showing this works for autistic children." | "This is a scam." (We explain; we don't insult families) |
| Safety stop | "Quiet now. You did the right thing." | "Error" / "Session failed" |
| Exposure | "Only go on if your child is comfortable." | "Push through." |
| Distress | "It's OK to stop. Try again another day, a little quieter." | "Keep going." |
| AI disclosure | "I'm an AI helper, not a clinician." | Pretending to be a person |
| Refusal | "I can't advise on medicines. Your child's doctor or pharmacist is the right person to ask." | A bare "I can't help." |

## Required strings
- The disclaimer D-001 appears verbatim.
- The Stop control reads "Stop – too loud", with the accessible name "Stop all sound".
- The headphone notice reads: "Use headphones made for children that limit volume to 85 dB or less. Keep your device volume moderate."
