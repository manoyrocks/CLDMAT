# AI Evaluation Report: Phase 3 (revised after rounds 2–3)

> **Read this first.** The round-1 results below (all 100%) were measured on sets written by the same agent that built and tuned the coach, and they were **overfit**. On blind sets written by independent evaluator agents, the deterministic coach does **not** meet the safety targets:
>
> | Blind set | When | Crisis escalation | Refusal | Injection | Grounded | Off-topic | Stimming policy | Cure claims |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | v1 (200 q) | Round 2, before fixes | **2/30 (6.7%)** | 35% | 55% | 39% | 90% | 20% | 0 |
> | v2 (165 q) | Round 2, untouched, after the classifier rebuild | **46.7%** | 63.3% | 73.3% | 71.7% | 90% | 50% | 0 |
> | v3 (170 q) | Round 3, untouched, after further fixes | **48.6%** | 80% | 86.7% | 68.3% | 95% | 60% | 0 |
>
> After being used for fixes, v1 and v2 became development sets; the coach scores 100% on their safety categories and they guard against regressions in `npm run eval`. The untouched v2 and v3 results are frozen in `results_round2_final_test.md` and `results_round3_final_test.md`.
>
> **Conclusion (ADR-0007):** a keyword classifier levels off at about half of unseen crises. Paraphrase, other languages (Malay, Mandarin, Hindi, Tamil, Bahasa Indonesia) and crises described only through actions defeat it. The **Gate 3 criterion "AI refusal and grounding targets met" is NOT met.** The coach is behind `VITE_FEATURE_COACH` and is **off in pilot builds** (`npm run build:pilot`) until, with the model-based `SafetyScreen` enabled and live-evaluated, a fresh blind set (including human-written items) reaches 100% crisis escalation and refusal. Cure claims stayed at **0** in every run, because answers are copied from approved text.

## 2. What changed during evaluation (and why that matters)
The first run missed three targets. Each was fixed at its root cause, never by relaxing a target:
1. **Unknown handling at 80%:** off-topic questions matched on one shared word. Fix: an IDF-weighted query-coverage threshold (≥ 0.25) plus BM25 ≥ 4.0.
2. **Grounding at 90% after that fix:** real parent phrasings ("afraid of sounds", "keep up", "vacuum gradually") were missing from the passage keywords. Fix: the keywords were enriched.
3. **Compliance false block:** a red-flag question legitimately asks about "retrain hearing" claims. Fix: a term approval scoped to that one question.

**Caveat (important):** steps 1–2 tuned retrieval against the same set that measures it, so the 100% grounding figure is optimistic. The Evaluators' judgment is that the safety metrics (refusal, escalation, injection, cure claims) are robust, because they come from deterministic rules that run *before* retrieval and are independent of this tuning. **(This judgment was wrong: blind sets later showed crisis escalation at 47–49%. See the top of this report.)** Grounding and unknown-handling need an **independent held-out set** before the pilot.

## 3. Design properties that make these results hold
- The safety pipeline is deterministic and ordered: crisis → injection → refusal → stimming policy → retrieval.
- In extractive mode, answer text is copied verbatim from approved passages, so hallucination is impossible by construction. Only retrieval *choice* can be wrong.
- The cure refusal quotes the approved disclaimer D-001. That is the only place a banned term may appear, and the metric excludes it by exact match.
- The coach receives only the question text: never the profile, logs or nickname. Questions are not stored (e2e verified).

## 4. LLM mode (opt-in, not enabled)
- Implemented: the Claude adapter (`packages/ai/src/llm-claude.ts`, server-side only, with refusal fallbacks), the system prompt that treats passages as data, and deterministic post-checks (banned terms, citation allow-list, dosage patterns, length). Any failure falls back to the extractive answer.
- Unit-tested with a mocked client: all 6 failure modes fall back, and rule-handled questions never reach the model.
- **Not evaluated live** (no credentials in this environment). Before enabling it: run this eval in LLM mode, plus an LLM-judge tone and grounding rubric, and confirm the post-check replacement rate is acceptable. This is a Gate 3 OUTSTANDING item.

## 5. Limitations and next steps
1. An independent red-team and held-out set (≥ 200 prompts, including Filipino, Singlish and code-switched phrasing) written by people who did not build the coach.
2. A safeguarding lead to review the crisis lexicon and regional numbers (OQ-12). The lexicon approach favours recall, but paraphrased crises may be missed. The escalation copy should be user-tested.
3. Multilingual evaluation when the phase-2 languages arrive.
