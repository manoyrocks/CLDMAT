## Banned claims

Terms: `cure`, `cures`, `cured`, `curing`, `heal`, `heals`, `healed`, `healing`, `rewire`, `rewires`, `rewired`, `rewiring`, `recover`, `recovers`, `recovered`, `recovery`, `reverse autism`, `reverses autism`, `normalise`, `normalize`, `retrain the brain`, `retrains the brain`, `retrain hearing`, `proven to`, `guaranteed`, `clinically proven`, `miracle`, `detox`

- Approved content may contain a banned term only inside an approved negation (claim marked approvedNegation, or an item with an Evaluator-recorded negation approval).
- AI-generated text may never contain a banned term. Questions about a cure are answered with the approved refusal template, which quotes D-001.
- No content may say or imply that the app or music reduces core autism severity (C-002).
