# Persona Research Plan

Owner: UX/UI Designers with Evaluators · Execution needs humans (recruitment, consent, sessions). Agents prepared the plan; **no research sessions have been run yet.**

## Objectives
1. Test the persona assumptions in team prompt §3: time poverty, music confidence, one-handed use, and the child's communication range.
2. Find the sound situations families find hardest (to seed M5 content).
3. Check that Child Mode is predictable and calming, with autistic adults as advisers.
4. Check that the microcopy is clear, affirming and free of any implied cure.

## Participants

| Group | n (min) | Recruitment | Inclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parents or guardians of autistic children aged 2–12 | 12 (6 SG, 6 PH) | Parent support groups, therapy centres | Mix of speaking and minimally verbal children; mix of music confidence |
| Credentialed music therapists | 4 | National associations (for example AMTAS Singapore, PMTA Philippines, AMTA, BAMT) | Current credential |
| Allied professionals (SLT, OT, teacher) | 4 | Clinics and schools | — |
| Autistic adult advisers | 4, paid | Autistic-led organisations | Lived experience of sound sensitivity preferred |
| Children (observed use of Child Mode, with the caregiver present) | 6 | From the parent group, with assent | Only after ethics review; stop at any sign of distress |

## Methods
- Semi-structured interviews (45 min) with a diary study of sound triggers over one week.
- Moderated usability tests of the prototype (the Phase 2 MVP build) with think-aloud, SUS and SEQ.
- Advisory review sessions with autistic adults, using the design system and Child Mode.
- Card sort for the information architecture of the Parent Mode menu.

## Ethics and safety
- Ethics or IRB review before child observation (OQ-08). Written informed consent from caregivers; the child's assent (verbal, AAC or behavioural) is respected at all times.
- Audio in Child Mode sessions runs at the default level (-20 dBFS); graded exposure is **not** tested with children in research.
- Data is pseudonymised; recordings are deleted after analysis.

## Outputs to feed back
Validated personas, a top-10 sound-trigger list, a usability defect list, and microcopy revisions. Results go into `/design` and change requirements through ADRs.
